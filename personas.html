Personas

One change can create very different experiences. Explore a fictional starting library, compare practical circumstances, and test the questions CEAM+ should ask.

Personas are tools for asking better questions. They do not predict how every person from a particular background will think, behave, or respond.

All profiles are fictional. This is an expandable starting set, not a representation of every population or a set of validated research personas. No real assessment participants are used.

Create a custom persona

Use a fictional composite of circumstances. Do not enter a real name, employer, address, contact details, health history, or identifiable story. A neutral scenario label is assigned automatically.

Fields are optional. Use “Unknown / not specified” or “Prefer not to answer” for sensitive details. Custom personas are user-created planning aids, separate from the CEAM+ library.

Anyone using this browser profile can read saved personas. If saving is blocked, work stays in this tab and can be exported. Assessment storage is separate.

Internal representation review · coverage, not validation

This review counts only the 16 CEAM+ library profiles, excluding custom scenarios. Counts show concentration; they are not population estimates or validated diversity targets. A single profile cannot speak for a community.

Perspectives still missing or insufficiently explored

Oceania, Central Asia, the Middle East, displaced and stateless people, people without stable housing, children’s direct perspectives, Deaf sign-language users, people with multiple access needs, informal agricultural labor, and people with no usable internet are not adequately represented. African, Asian, European, Latin American, and Caribbean settings each cover very few circumstances. No specific Tribal Nation is represented; community-led co-design is needed before making locally specific claims.

All profiles lack lived-experience review. The provisional Indigenous-community profile is a prompt to seek locally authorized review, not evidence of community views or approval.

How personas are created, reviewed, and retired

Start with a question and explicit circumstances, record provenance and unknowns, invite relevant lived-experience review with consent, and test for counterexamples. Version updates and retire misleading profiles without silently changing old exports. Representation is not validation.
