About Faye Briggs

My name is Faye Briggs. I am a first-generation college graduate, McNair Scholar, MBA student, future PhD student, researcher, mother, entrepreneur, and creator of CEAM+.

Much of my life has been spent learning how to navigate systems on my own. That shaped the way I see people, work, education, technology, and support.

Where this perspective comes from

I have lived through family court systems, therapy programs, rehabilitation programs, group homes, homelessness, recovery, workforce challenges, higher education, and small business environments.

Those experiences changed how I look at success. I became interested in why some people are able to thrive while others struggle inside the same environment. I wanted to understand what makes a system supportive, what makes it confusing, and what happens when people are expected to adapt without enough guidance.

People

I pay attention to how people learn, respond to pressure, ask for help, and rebuild after difficult seasons.

Behavior

I care about what people actually do when they are overwhelmed, unsupported, or trying to change.

Systems

I look at the environment around the person, not just the person standing inside it.

Technology

I want AI and digital tools to make life clearer, not harder to understand.

Education changed what I believed was possible

Becoming a mother changed my priorities. It made the future feel more urgent. I wanted to build something better, not only for myself but for my family and for other people trying to find their way through difficult systems.

Education transformed my life. It gave me language for things I had already lived. Business helped me understand operations and decision making. Psychology helped me understand behavior and adaptation. Technology and AI helped me see how quickly systems are changing.

The McNair Scholars Program helped me realize I was capable of research and that pursuing a PhD was not out of reach. It helped me see that my lived experience, questions, and academic work could belong in the same conversation.

How CEAM+ began

My research on AI adoption in small businesses led me to a pattern I could not ignore.

  1. 01

    The issue was not only technology

    People were not always rejecting AI because they were against it. Many were overwhelmed, unsure, or unsupported.

  2. 02

    The same barriers kept showing up

    Too much information, too many choices, unclear communication, lack of trust, and confusing systems made adoption harder.

  3. 03

    CEAM+ became a structure

    I created CEAM+ to connect cognition, ethics, adoption, and ongoing improvement in a way people could actually use.

What I am building toward

My goal is not simply to help organizations use AI. My goal is to help people understand systems, reduce overwhelm, improve decision making, and create better futures for themselves and their families.

My long-term vision is to earn a PhD in Information Systems and Operations Management, continue researching human-centered AI adoption, build ClearPathway Systems, and help organizations create environments where people can learn, adapt, and succeed.

CEAM+ is part of that vision. It is a way to ask better questions before adding new tools, to make support more visible, and to help people move from confusion to confidence one step at a time.
