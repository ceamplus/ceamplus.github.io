About Faye Briggs and ClearPathway Systems

My name is Faye Briggs. I am a first-generation college graduate, McNair Scholar, MBA student, future PhD student, researcher, entrepreneur, and creator of CEAM+.

Much of my life has been spent learning how to navigate systems on my own. That shaped the way I see people, work, education, technology, and support.

ClearPathway Systems grew from that experience. CEAM+ became the framework we use to help people and small businesses understand complex situations, make better decisions, identify where AI or another tool fits, and build the support needed for real change.

How CEAM+ is evolving

CEAM+ began with research into AI adoption in small businesses. The same barriers kept appearing beyond technology: overload, emotion, trust, limited resources, environmental pressure, uncertainty, unclear roles, and difficulty sustaining action.

That insight expanded CEAM+ into a Human Adaptation and Decision-Making Framework. AI literacy remains important, but the larger purpose is helping people understand challenges, make thoughtful choices, act with agency, and improve over time.

Technology does not create change by itself. People create change through understanding, trust, emotions, choices, behaviors, environments, and consistent action.

Where this perspective comes from

I have lived through family court systems, therapy programs, rehabilitation programs, group homes, homelessness, recovery, workforce challenges, higher education, and small business environments.

Those experiences changed how success looked to me. I started paying attention to why some people are able to thrive while others struggle inside the same environment. I wanted to understand what makes a system supportive, what makes it confusing, and what happens when people are expected to adapt without enough guidance.

That question sits at the center of ClearPathway Systems. We look at the person, the environment, the decision, and the tool together. We do not assume technology is the answer before we understand what people are carrying.

What we pay attention to

ClearPathway Systems looks at the full picture: the person, the system, the pressure around the decision, and the support needed to make change realistic.

People

We pay attention to how people learn, respond to pressure, ask for help, and rebuild after difficult seasons.

Behavior

We care about what people actually do when they are overwhelmed, unsupported, or trying to change.

Systems

We look at the environment around the person, not just the person standing inside it.

Technology

We want AI and digital tools to make life clearer, not harder to understand.

Education changed what felt possible

Education changed my priorities. It made the future feel more possible and gave me a clearer way to understand the systems I had already lived through. I wanted to build something useful for people trying to find their way through difficult systems.

Education transformed my life. It gave me language for things I had already lived. Business helped me understand operations and decision making. Psychology helped me understand behavior and adaptation. Technology and AI helped me see how quickly systems are changing.

The McNair Scholars Program helped me realize I was capable of research and that pursuing a PhD was not out of reach. It helped me see that my lived experience, questions, and academic work could belong in the same conversation.

That belief is part of how we work now. ClearPathway Systems is built around the idea that people can learn and adapt when systems are explained clearly, support is visible, and change is introduced in a way people can actually use.

How CEAM+ began

My research on AI adoption in small businesses led me to a pattern I could not ignore. People were not always rejecting AI because they were against it. Many were overwhelmed, unsure, or unsupported.

CEAM+ became a way to organize those barriers into something useful. Its expanded layers now connect cognition, emotion, agency, trust and ethics, environment, implementation, human-AI partnership, growth and meaning, and continuous improvement.

The issue was not only technology

People often need clearer steps, better support, and time to understand where a tool fits.

The same barriers kept showing up

Too much information, too many choices, unclear communication, and lack of trust made adoption harder.

CEAM+ became a structure

The framework helps us turn confusion into a practical path people and organizations can follow.

What we are building toward

Our goal is not simply to help people use AI. The deeper goal is to help individuals and small businesses understand systems, reduce overwhelm, improve decision making, adopt beneficial changes, and create better futures.

My long-term vision is to earn a PhD in Information Systems and Operations Management and continue researching human-centered AI adoption. ClearPathway Systems carries that work forward by helping organizations create environments where people can learn, adapt, and succeed.

CEAM+ is one part of that vision. It gives us a way to ask better questions before adding new tools, make support more visible, and help people move from confusion to confidence one step at a time.
