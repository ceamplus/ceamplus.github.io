Potential measures

  • Customer retention
  • Customer lifetime value
  • Customer profitability
  • Customer acquisition

Connect activity to outcomes.

Examine where marketing effort goes, how customers respond, and which comparisons are meaningful.

  • Channel performance

  • ROMI

ROMI: return on marketing investment. ROCI: return on customer investment. Definitions and attribution assumptions would be agreed for each pilot.

04–06 / Analysis & decision support

Move from observation to options.

These are proposed areas for pilot development. Choose methods that fit the available data and the decision being considered.

Diagnose

Diagnostic Analytics

Investigate relationships and possible drivers behind changes in performance. An association is a starting point for inquiry, not proof of causation.

  • Why did sales decline?
  • Why did repeat purchasing change?
  • Which customer segments changed?
  • Which channels contributed most to performance changes?
Predict

Predictive Analytics

Identify patterns that may help estimate future outcomes when data quality and validation support the analysis.

  • Customer retention risk
  • Potential demand patterns
  • Customer value trends
  • Sales forecasting

Predictions represent estimates and should not be treated as certainties. Evaluate uncertainty, assumptions, and performance as conditions change.

Prescribe

Prescriptive Decision Support

Help compare possible actions rather than automatically dictate decisions. Human reviewers weigh tradeoffs and decide which options deserve a test.

  • Which customer segments may deserve additional attention?
  • Where might marketing resources be better allocated?
  • Which retention strategies should be tested?
  • Which signals warrant management review?
07 / The CEAM+ perspective

CEAM+ Signals

Business performance has a human context. Consider visible and less visible indicators alongside customer, marketing, and organizational data.

  • Trust
  • Engagement
  • Resistance
  • Adoption
  • Customer behavior change
  • Human agency
  • Overdependence
  • Critical-thinking erosion
  • Burnout or friction
  • Adaptive capacity
  • Early warning signals

These signals are contextual indicators, not deterministic predictions of human behavior. They are not live scores in this example. A pilot would define appropriate measures and examine self-report, observation, context, and alternative explanations together.

CEAM+ does not assume every person follows the same pathway or reaches the same outcome. Resistance may signal poor fit, overload, or an ethical concern; adoption alone does not establish willingness or benefit.

More Than Reporting

Traditional dashboards often stop at reporting what happened. CEAM+ is being developed to connect the numbers to context and possible responses.

  1. Data
  2. Patterns
  3. Context
  4. Human and organizational signals
  5. Decision options

A dashboard may show that sales declined. CEAM+ is designed to help users investigate which customer, marketing, organizational, and human signals may be contributing to that change and determine which responses are worth testing.

AI may assist with pattern recognition, analytics, summarization, forecasting, and decision support. People remain responsible for reviewing evidence and making decisions. Explore AI within CEAM+

Practical development

CEAM+ Pilot Approach

CEAM+ is being developed through practical pilots. Each pilot would connect a real decision need with suitable data, methods, and human interpretation.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Understand the organization, customers, available data, and current decision environment.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Identify meaningful business, customer, marketing, and human-centered indicators.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Use descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics where appropriate.

  4. 04

    Interpret

    Place quantitative results within the organization’s real-world context.

  5. 05

    Test

    Recommend small, measurable interventions rather than sweeping changes.

  6. 06

    Learn

    Measure outcomes and refine the system.

The path forward connects the CEAM+ framework, the dashboard, business insights and decision support, and pilot implementations. Future productized services or SaaS capabilities may follow as the approach is tested. This is an evolving platform, not a mature enterprise SaaS product.

Start with a decision worth understanding.

The broader CEAM+ framework studies how people, organizations, customers, and markets adopt, resist, sustain, and adapt to change. Marketing is the first application, with agency, resilience, ethical judgment, trust, sustainability, adaptive capacity, and human value at its foundation.
