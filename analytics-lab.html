Quick start · Market growth and purchasers

Financial dashboard · Available calculated measures

Revenue is not cash flow. Margin differs from markup. ROAS measures attributed revenue per advertising spend; ROMI depends on incremental revenue or contribution assumptions, and ROI uses the explicitly defined return and investment. This is a decision-support view, not accounting software.

Marketing budget planner

Enter a planned allocation to see totals and percentages. Add actual spending only when it uses the same period and scope; a variance needs context before it is called favorable or unfavorable.

Methodology · Formulas, assumptions, and alternatives

Statistical methods follow standard sample mean, Student t, Welch, correlation, and least-squares definitions. NIST: confidence limits for the mean. OpenStax: break-even analysis.

Beyond the Dashboard · Python, SQL, and future AI

The same group comparisons can be expressed with pandas groupby, mean, sum, and corr, or SQL AVG(), SUM(), COUNT(), and GROUP BY. A future AI interpretation service would require a secure server endpoint, explicit data-sharing choices, and human review. No AI service runs in this version.

This persona is a planning tool and should not replace actual customer research. Persona generation is not active and synthetic personas are not part of the analytical evidence.

Limitations & privacy

CEAM+ supports analysis and human judgment for educational, exploratory, and business decision-support purposes. Results depend on the quality and meaning of the data. Correlation does not prove causation; statistical findings need context. Uncertainty cannot be removed by adding more frameworks.

Files remain in this browser tab. The local parser does not execute spreadsheet formulas or macros; Excel workbooks use saved cell results. No uploaded contents are sent to a server. Use a current browser and small, relevant datasets without unnecessary personal or sensitive information. This tool is not designed as a protected personal-data repository.

Numeric parsing supports decimal points and comma thousands separators; ambiguous regional formats require normalization. Tests require suitable sampling and measurement. Forecasts and planning ratings are estimates and discussion aids. Any future AI-generated interpretation may contain errors. Synthetic personas cannot substitute for customer research.

Attribution: Observed sales following marketing activity do not necessarily mean the marketing activity caused all of those sales. Examine baseline sales, controls, experiments, incrementality, and attribution assumptions.
