CEAM+ Framework

CEAM+ is centered on one stable research identity: Understanding how people and organizations adopt, resist, and sustain change in an increasingly AI-driven world.

Technology will change. Research questions will evolve. The core mission remains stable, so future tools can be studied and incorporated without replacing the framework's purpose.

Foundational Anchors

Permanent concepts that define CEAM+: research identity, Conditions for Change, agency, ethical clarity, and sustained adaptation.

Research Library

Supporting theories, models, papers, and literature that inform CEAM+ without becoming core components.

Explorations

New ideas being evaluated before deciding whether they belong in the framework.

Research Agenda

Living research questions organized by timeless, decade, and emerging questions while the identity remains stable.

Conditions for Change

Conditions for Change are the conditions that influence a person's or organization's readiness and capacity for change. They do not decide what someone should choose. They help explain why some choices feel possible, difficult, risky, or sustainable.

Time

Individuals need time to learn, practice, recover from mistakes, and fit change into real life. Organizations need timelines that allow training, review, adoption, and adjustment instead of rushed rollout.

Resources

Individuals may need devices, internet access, money, transportation, documentation, or trusted help. Organizations need staffing, budgets, data quality, infrastructure, policies, and implementation support.

Cognitive Capacity

Individuals make better choices when instructions, steps, and decisions do not overload attention. Organizations need workflows, dashboards, and communication that reduce confusion rather than adding another layer of complexity.

Emotional Readiness

Individuals may carry fear, skepticism, fatigue, hope, or pressure into change. Organizations also have emotional climates shaped by trust, burnout, past failed initiatives, leadership credibility, and psychological safety.

Social Environment

Individuals are influenced by family, peers, managers, teachers, care teams, and community norms. Organizations are shaped by culture, incentives, leadership behavior, informal networks, and whether people feel safe asking questions.

Habits & Routine

Individuals are more likely to sustain change when it fits existing rhythms or replaces a clear pain point. Organizations need change to fit meetings, handoffs, documentation habits, accountability systems, and daily operating routines.

Agency

Individuals and organizations need meaningful choices they can recognize and act on. Agency connects conditions to decisions, action, learning, adaptation, and sustained change.

Agency

Agency is not the absence of barriers. It is the ability to recognize and act within the meaningful choices that remain despite those barriers.

In CEAM+, conditions influence people. Agency guides responses. CEAM+ helps people understand conditions rather than telling them what to choose.

Conditions for Change → Agency → Choices → Actions → Learning → Adaptation → Sustained Change

This sequence keeps decision-making visible. The goal is not to remove every barrier before movement begins, but to understand the conditions clearly enough to make intentional choices inside them.

Research Agenda

The CEAM+ research identity remains stable while its questions keep evolving. This structure keeps long-term inquiry separate from questions shaped by current tools, markets, policies, and AI capabilities.

Level 1 - Timeless Questions

How do people and organizations adopt, resist, and sustain change? What makes a choice feel possible? How do trust, learning, support, identity, and environment shape adaptation over time?

Level 2 - Decade Questions

How are current work, education, healthcare, rehabilitation, and community systems changing as AI becomes common? What organizational practices help people adapt without increasing overwhelm?

Level 3 - Emerging Questions

What new questions are created by generative AI, automation, personalization, agentic tools, changing labor patterns, data systems, and new forms of human-machine decision-making?

Applied Contexts

CEAM+ can be used in personal life, work, education, healthcare, rehabilitation, and future settings that do not yet exist. The context changes, but the framework keeps asking how conditions, agency, choices, learning, and adaptation shape sustained change.

Personal / Daily Life

Use AI support for routines, reminders, planning, paperwork, communication, and realistic goal tracking.

Work / Business

Evaluate AI opportunities, job fit, workflow efficiency, and readiness before scaling.

Learning / Education

Support responsible learning tools, accessibility, communication preferences, and student-centered safeguards.

Health / Care Settings

Map personal, clinical, or administrative AI use against risk, usability, and accountability needs.

Rehabilitation / Adaptive Support

Shape support tools around personal goals, daily needs, communication style, and trusted care.
