# CEAM+ Version 1 Refocus Design

## Goal

Refocus the public CEAM+ experience on small business owners, everyday individuals, AI beginners, personal growth, productivity, better decisions, technology adoption, and successful implementation.

## Preservation Boundary

All existing pages, assessments, questions, scoring logic, theories, framework content, webhook integration, and support-request functionality remain intact. Version 1 changes the public presentation and emphasis, not the underlying research foundation or assessment system.

## Homepage

The homepage leads with "Turning Awareness Into Action." and two direct paths: Start Assessment and Explore Guides. It then explains why CEAM+ exists, introduces the five human-centered layers, separates personal and small-business use cases, states the core philosophy, and closes with a single assessment-focused call to action.

The visual direction retains the existing CEAM+ logo, forest green, mint, blue, gold, and coral palette. The layout uses open sections, restrained bordered cards, plain language, and responsive two-column patterns. It avoids academic framing, consulting language, fake metrics, and technology-first imagery.

## Mission Page

The mission page explains what CEAM+ is, why it was created, the implementation gap it addresses, the value it provides, and how the five layers help. Existing human-first values and concepts remain on the page. Education, healthcare, rehabilitation, communities, and larger organizations are retained as future applications rather than the primary Version 1 audience.

## Assessment Email

The client assessment-results email and support-request confirmation email end with:

> For follow-up support, contact support at briggsfaye@clearpathtechnologies.org.

Admin messages, Zapier fields, recipient routing, and assessment data remain unchanged.

## Expanded Human Adaptation Framework

The public five-layer model remains the beginner-friendly entry point. The deeper framework expands into nine layers: Cognitive, Emotional, Agency, Trust & Ethics, Environment, Adoption & Implementation, Human-AI Partnership, Growth & Meaning, and CEAM+ Continuous Improvement.

Existing assessment questions remain in place. The previous Plus questions become the continuous-improvement layer, and additional plain-language questions cover emotion, agency, human-AI roles, purpose, and values. Personal and small-business examples lead the educational material, while education, healthcare, rehabilitation, community, and larger-organization applications remain available as future uses.
