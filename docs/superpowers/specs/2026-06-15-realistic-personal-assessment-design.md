# Realistic Personal Assessment Design

## Goal

Keep the comprehensive CEAM+ Personal / Daily Life assessment while making every question feel grounded in real personal circumstances rather than adapted business language.

## Audience and Tone

The assessment is for adults navigating ordinary life pressures, including children, partners, relatives, caregiving, work demands, money, household responsibilities, health routines, learning, and personal goals.

Questions use plain, respectful, non-diagnostic wording. They acknowledge fear, shame, exhaustion, grief, conflict, avoidance, relapse into old habits, and loss of confidence without labeling the person or treating a setback as failure.

## Content Model

The assessment retains all nine CEAM+ layers and approximately 58 questions:

- Cognitive: competing demands, mental load, decision fatigue, forgotten details, and work-family spillover.
- Emotional: fear of failure, shame, frustration, guilt, grief, anger, and confidence.
- Agency: control, influence, boundaries, asking for help, and restarting with one manageable action.
- Trust and Ethics: privacy, family information, money, health, children, and verifying personal advice.
- Environment: children, family expectations, work schedules, caregiving, conflict, noise, transportation, and available support.
- AI Solution and Implementation: practical personal goals, affordability, ease of use, and realistic learning capacity.
- Human-AI Partnership: what AI may help organize and what must remain a personal or professional judgment.
- Growth and Meaning: family, stability, independence, purpose, self-respect, and future goals.
- Continuous Improvement: triggers, effects, recovery after setbacks, relapse patterns, self-compassion, and sustainable restarting.

## Behavioral Compatibility

- Preserve assessment ID, question IDs, phase IDs, scoring behavior, result structure, email templates, and Zapier fields.
- Personal labels and options are supplied through the existing category override system.
- Full answers, notes, and follow-up responses remain included in both client and administrator emails.
- Business, education, healthcare, and rehabilitation assessments remain unchanged.

## Recommendation Quality

Personal answers should expose practical patterns for future recommendations and guidebooks:

- pressure sources
- setback triggers
- effects of setbacks
- restart preferences
- family and work demands
- support availability
- privacy concerns
- goals and values

- Confirm every shared question template has a personal override.
- Confirm option and score-array lengths match.
- Confirm 58 personal questions across all nine layers.
- Confirm no business-only terms appear in the rendered personal assessment.
- Confirm full-answer email output includes all answered questions, notes, and follow-ups.
