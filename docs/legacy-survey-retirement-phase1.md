# Legacy survey entry points

October 20, 2026

The standalone survey entry points are retired. Following the integration update, their original questionnaires are available within Assessment Center as self-report assessments. See [the integration report](self-report-assessment-integration.md) for all eighteen questionnaires, preserved methods and separate new history.

Both old URLs remain available with read-only historical records and original downloads. No saved legacy record is converted, renamed, rescored or merged with assessment trends. The original engines and shared-script functions remain intact; the new Assessment Center interface reuses the context engine locally without mounting its email submission workflow. Phase 2 deletion and shared-script extraction are not included.
